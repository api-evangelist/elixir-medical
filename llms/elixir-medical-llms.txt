\<li\>16% increase in blood flow\</li\>

\<li\>66% reduction in TLF at 4 years vs\. DES\</li\>
\</ul\>

A prospective, multicenter, single\-blind study evaluating the safety and efficacy of DESyne BDS Plus\.
\<br\>
\<b\>Study results:\</b\>
\<ul\>
\<li\>Achieved primary endpoint of TLF noninferiority\</li\>
\<li\>No stent thrombosis \(definite/probable\), CVD, or TV\-MI in the DESyne BDS Plus arm\</li\>
\<li\>Sustained low TLF \& TVF rates from 12 months to 24 months\</li\>
\</ul\>

A post\-market study to evaluate the safety and performance of LithiX™ HC\-IVL in a real\-world population with highly complex calcified lesions\.

\<li\>113\.71% overall mean stent expansion\</li\>
