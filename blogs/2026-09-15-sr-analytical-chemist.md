Major Duties and Responsibilities Responsibilities will include, but not be limited to: Perform analytical chemistry research to develop robust assays based on new and existing methodologies. Develop, establish, and validate testing methods used to control raw materials, in-process/production intermediates, and final products. Establish, validate, and document new or existing compound identification methods.
